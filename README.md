# 🪔 Happy Diwali - Wishes & Celebration Website 🪔

A beautiful, interactive website designed to celebrate Diwali with 10 meaningful steps to complete, featuring an animated Diya (lamp) that glows brighter as you progress!

## ✨ Features

- **10 Interactive Steps**: Complete meaningful Diwali celebration activities
  1. Spread Love
  2. Spread Happiness
  3. Light a Diya
  4. Share Sweets
  5. Clean & Decorate
  6. Wear New Clothes
  7. Burst Firecrackers
  8. Pray & Bless
  9. Play Games
  10. Share Wishes

- **Animated Diya**: Watch the lamp's flame grow brighter as you complete more steps
- **Progress Tracking**: Visual progress bar showing your completion status
- **Completion Celebration**: Confetti and victory sounds when all steps are complete
- **Persistent Progress**: Your progress is saved in browser's localStorage
- **Responsive Design**: Beautiful on all devices - desktop, tablet, and mobile
- **Diwali Theme**: Golden and warm colors with festive animations

## 🎨 Design Highlights

- **Gradient backgrounds** with Diwali colors (gold, orange, dark blue)
- **Animated flame** that intensifies with progress
- **Glowing effects** using CSS shadows and animations
- **Smooth transitions** and interactive hover effects
- **Particle effects** when completing steps
- **Confetti celebration** when all tasks are done

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Click on each step card to mark it as completed
3. Watch the Diya's flame grow brighter with each step
4. Complete all 10 steps to see the celebration!
5. Press 'R' to reset and celebrate again

## 📁 Files

- `index.html` - Main HTML structure with 10 steps
- `style.css` - Beautiful Diwali-themed styling
- `script.js` - Interactive functionality and animations

## 🎵 Features Included

- ✅ Click checkboxes to mark steps complete
- ✅ Progress bar updates in real-time
- ✅ Animated Diya grows brighter with progress
- ✅ Sound effects when completing steps (Web Audio API)
- ✅ Particle effects on step completion
- ✅ Full celebration with confetti when done
- ✅ Auto-save progress to localStorage
- ✅ Reset button or press 'R' key to start again

## 🌟 Customization

You can easily customize:
- Step descriptions in `index.html`
- Colors in `style.css` (search for `#ffd700` for gold, `#ff6b00` for orange)
- Animation speeds (change duration values in CSS)
- Sound frequencies in `script.js`
